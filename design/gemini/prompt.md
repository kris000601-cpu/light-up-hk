# Pictogram brief: Hong Kong travel-passport stamps

## The task

I am making a travel-passport app for Hong Kong. When a user visits a place, they get an octagonal entry stamp for it. On the left side of each stamp is a small line pictogram of that place.

Please draw these pictograms, **one place per image**, following the list at the end of this brief.

The attached image `reference-clock.png` (the Tsim Sha Tsui Clock Tower) is the finished reference. Every pictogram must look like it belongs to the same set as this reference: same line weight, same construction, same base, same amount of detail.

## Why the reference works (copy this thinking)

1. It draws **one concrete, recognisable thing** (that specific clock tower), not an idea.
2. There is **one hero subject**, centred and upright, filling most of the height. The low buildings beside it and the water lines under it are small and only support it.
3. The subject is **built from simple geometric shapes stacked from top to bottom**: a triangle spire, a small box, then a tall box. It is symmetrical.
4. It has **one or two small identifying details** (the clock face and the arched door). Each detail is clearly smaller than the main forms.
5. It sits on a **fixed base**: a ground line, then two rows of waves, the second row shorter.
6. It has **few lines and lots of empty space**.

## Style rules (follow every one, for every image)

- **One subject only.** Centred. It fills about 80% of the image height.
- **Flat elevation, no perspective, no 3D.** Use a front view. For boats, animals, bridges and vehicles, use a flat side view.
- **Build the subject from simple geometric shapes**: rectangles, triangles, circles, arcs, straight lines. Use left–right symmetry wherever the real subject allows.
- **One or two small identifying details only.** No other decoration.
- **Uniform line weight**, identical to the reference (about 3% of the image width). Rounded line caps and rounded joins.
- **Outlines only.** No fills, no solid black areas, no shading, no hatching, no gradients, no texture, no hand-drawn wobble, no sketchy double lines.
- **One colour**: #1E2B3C lines on a pure white background.
- **The base, same in every image:**
  - A straight horizontal ground line at about 80% of the image height. At most one or two very low, simple shapes may sit on it beside the subject.
  - Below the ground line, two rows of lines, the second row shorter than the first.
  - If the place is marked **[sea]**, these two rows are gentle wave lines, exactly as in the reference.
  - If the place is marked **[land]**, they are two straight horizontal lines.
- **Lots of empty space** around the drawing. Square 1:1, 1024 × 1024 px.
- It must stay **clear and recognisable when shrunk to 66 px wide**. Prefer fewer, bolder forms over many small lines.
- **Never include:**
  - text, letters, numbers, Chinese characters, or signs with writing;
  - people;
  - floating extras such as sun, moon, clouds, birds, stars, sparkles or motion lines.
- **Animals:** an animal may appear only when the place's description below names it as the subject.

## How to deliver

- **One image per reply.** Go through the list in order, starting with s001.
- Before each image, write one line with the place's ID and name, for example: `s001 太平山頂 · 獅子亭`.
- After each image, wait for my reply.
  - If I write **"next"**, draw the next place on the list.
  - If I write a correction, redraw the same place with that correction applied.
- Keep everything in this same conversation, so that the style stays consistent across the whole set.
- If a description below is not accurate for the real place, keep the style rules and draw the most recognisable real feature of that place instead. Say in one line what you changed.
